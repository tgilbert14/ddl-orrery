## The Hub Visionary — senior MITHRIL, owner of the orbit hall: the hub, the HUD, the Artifact, arrival, and the frame that holds eleven worlds.

**Critique.** The hub's bones are genuinely good — a real z-sorted orbit (planetPos/drawSky in /home/user/ddl-orrery/src/05-core.js), an Artifact with a face-swapping state machine (SKIN_OF in /home/user/ddl-orrery/src/05c-sphereforge.js), one disciplined ticker — but the camera is dead: pointer parallax touches only the star loop (par.x * 14 * s.z), so the Artifact, the brass ring, and all eleven planets sit pinned while the stars slide, and the ellipse reads as a flat diagram the moment your eye asks for depth. The phone hub quietly abandons the whole premise: under 700px `.orbits` collapses to the brochure list (02-hub.css line 155), the sphere is 0.5-alpha scenery painted at H*0.72 behind a scrolling column, and `#artifact-hit` is `display:none !important` (03b-deco.css line 330) — the majority device never meets the character the site is named for, never sees a skin swap, never hears a preview. The Artifact answers only 7 of 11 worlds: SKIN_OF has no entries for archive, drillyard, stormwall, or beacons, and the WORLDS registry hands all four the same generic `tell: 'pulse'` — the four newest worlds hover mute at the flagship's own front door. Eleven nameplates on one ellipse survive on `:nth-child(odd)` side-alternation and `--pdim` dimming, which prevents stacking but not conjunctions when two planets pass close on the near arc. Onboarding is literally an instruction string — `.hub-hint` reads "Choose a world · Tab to cycle · Enter to travel" — on a project whose manifesto is show-don't-tell. And the warp, the moment of maximum drama, is one identical radial-gradient flood (`#warpfx` + `warp-flood`) for every destination; only the tint changes. The pieces are instrument-grade; the instrument just never moves under your hands.

## The signature moment

You press the golden sphere — and hold. The surface dents under your touch, the liquid-warp surging around your fingertip, and a single brass sonar ring detaches and begins to travel outward across the dark. It reaches the nearest world and the planet flares in its own color and sounds its own voice — one clear note in the Dust Sea's scale. The ring keeps going. Velocity answers pink and synthetic; the Grid answers with a glyph-cold pluck; each world it crosses swells, lights its nameplate for a heartbeat, and adds its note, so the orrery plays itself as a radial arpeggio in orbital order — eleven voices, struck by one expanding wave, the chord assembling in real time out of the same generative score that has been humming all along. The ring passes the last world and dissolves at the edge of the porthole glass, and in the silence after, the Artifact answers with its slightly-wrong 196-sagging-to-185 tone — as if it heard its worlds report in, and is satisfied. Nobody who does this once does it only once, and nobody explains the site again after seeing it: they just hand you the phone and say "press the sphere."

## The upgrades

- **The Camera Wakes — full-scene tilt, parallax, and depth-of-field** — *🌟 jaw-drop · effort M*
  Move your mouse (or tilt your phone) and the entire instrument answers: the ring tilts toward you, planets shear by depth, the Artifact leans a few pixels, far-side worlds soften into bokeh while near ones stay crisp. The hub stops being a diagram and becomes a place you are standing in.
  *How:* Promote `par` in 05-core.js into a shared camera: in drawSky, offset the nebula blits by par*4, ART.cx/cy by par*6, and each planet inside planetPos by par * (10 + 8*depth) so near worlds shear more than far ones — DOM labels inherit it for free because they already track --pax/--pay per frame. Modulate the ellipse in measureOrbit-space: draw the ring and compute planetPos with ry * (1 + par.y*0.15), so looking 'down' opens the orbit and looking level flattens it edge-on. DOF: at PlanetForge.init, bake one blur(2px) LOD sprite per world (ctx.filter at bake time only, never per frame); in drawWorld pick blurred vs sharp by p.depth < -0.45 with a short alpha crossfade band. Phone: feed deviceorientation beta/gamma into the same par.tx/ty (iOS requestPermission gated behind the first tap). rm holds the camera at 0 — the designed static pose survives untouched.

- **Sound the Orrery — hold the Artifact, play all eleven worlds** — *🌟 jaw-drop · effort M*
  Press and hold the sphere: a sonar ring sweeps outward and every world it crosses flares and sounds its note in its own voice — a radial arpeggio across the whole system, ending in the Artifact's slightly-wrong answering tone. The site's thesis (eleven worlds, one clock) becomes something you physically strum.
  *How:* pointerdown on #artifact-hit arms a 450ms hold timer (a quick click still does the existing ripple/reveal). On trigger, launch a dedicated sweep ring reusing SphereForge's rings pool math (R*1.05→3.2 over SONAR_MS); each frame in drawSky compare ring radius to every planet's elliptical distance d_i = hypot(p.x-ART.cx, (p.y-ART.cy)*(ART.rx/ART.ry)); on first crossing, stamp a per-world flareUntil read by drawWorld (reuse the hover swell +1.18 and full alpha for 600ms) and dispatch Orrery.events 'chime' {slug} — 07-audio.js answers with one playLead note in that world's rig voice, quantized to the next 16th so it always lands musically. Keyboard: hold Enter on the focused #artifact-hit. rm: no ring, notes fire in distance order on a fixed 90ms stagger and each nameplate ticks bright — the designed equivalent, not a stripped one.

- **The Pocket Orrery — phones get the real hub, not the brochure** — *🌟 jaw-drop · effort L*
  On a phone the Artifact holds mid-screen and the eleven worlds ride a tall ellipse around it. Swipe to spin the system with momentum; it snaps to the nearest world, whose name and tell settle into a brass plate under the sphere; tap to travel. The phone experience finally IS the orrery.
  *How:* In measureOrbit's phone branch (05-core.js line 183), stop zeroing rx/ry: set ART.cy=H*0.44, rx=W*0.36, ry=H*0.26 and let the existing z-sorted drawWorld path run under 700px. Add a `spin` offset to planetPos's ang, driven by touch drag on a full-screen listener (dx/W * PI per swipe) with decaying velocity on the Ticker and ease-snap so one world rests at depth=+1. Suppress in-orbit .pa-label on phones; instead a fixed .focus-plate (real DOM, brass chamfer idiom from 03b-deco.css) shows the snapped world's b/em text and is the tap target alongside the planet anchor itself (anchors already track --pax/--pay). Re-enable #artifact-hit at R*1.2 now that no list sits over it, and remove the mobile display:none at 03b-deco.css line 330. The stacked list remains untouched below the fold as the no-JS brochure and the AT-friendly index — the skip-link already points at #worlds-index.

- **The Artifact notices you — gaze, idle overtures, and all eleven faces** — *◆ strong · effort M*
  The sphere's wandering highlight drifts toward your cursor like an eye tracking you; linger near it and the near-side reflections brighten; ignore it for twenty seconds and it pings you first. And it finally answers ALL eleven worlds — hover the Archive or Stormwall and it wears their face like everyone else's.
  *How:* Pass pointer coords into SphereForge.draw via opts; lerp the wanderer's lissajous target toward the pointer projected onto the disc (clamped to r*0.6) at 0.04/frame, and scale each hots[] alpha by 1 + 0.25*dot(hotPos, pointerDir). Idle: a 20s timer in 05-core.js (reset on input, hub-only) fires SphereForge.ping() + the existing 'sonar' event — the score's blip already answers. Fix the mute worlds: extend SKIN_OF with archive/drillyard/stormwall/beacons — new webp renders if the art budget allows, else a zero-asset fallback in bandFor(): synthesize a 2048x512 band from PlanetForge's baked texture for that slug so setSkin never dead-ends at 'orrery'. Give the four new worlds real `tell` values in WORLDS while in there. Master surveyors (survey-complete flag already in store) get greeted on return: one unprompted 1.2s reveal() flash of the biomech underlayer.

- **Brass rails — the nav metaphor made of metal** — *◆ strong · effort M*
  Ghost-faint spokes run from the Artifact's hub to every world, and the ellipse becomes a double-etched rail with degree ticks. Hover a world and its spoke ignites in that world's hue, current flowing outward along it — a constellation line that doubles as a wayfinding beam, and the line the warp flood follows when you commit.
  *How:* All in drawSky's hub block (05-core.js ~line 250): per planet, one line from (ART.cx,ART.cy) to (p.x,p.y) at rgba(brass,0.04) — eleven strokes, near-free; the ellipse gains a second stroke at rx+6/ry+6 plus 24 short tick marks (precomputed angles, one path). On hovered===slug, restroke that spoke with a cached per-world linear gradient (bake 11 gradients at init, keyed by slug — zero per-frame createLinearGradient) and animate flow via setLineDash([3,9]) with lineDashOffset -= dt*0.02. travel() already aims --wx/--wy at the planet; add a 300ms bright rail flash before the flood so the eye reads 'energy leaves the hub, rides the rail, blooms into the world'. rm: spokes hold at rest alpha, hover lights the spoke statically with no dash-flow.

- **Nameplate declutter + the spyglass** — *◆ strong · effort M*
  Eleven names never collide again: far-side worlds drop to engraved ticks, the front rank keeps full nameplates, and whichever planet is nearest your cursor gets the spyglass — its plate swells with a hairline leader, its one-line tell already legible before you commit to hovering.
  *How:* drawWorld already writes --pdim per frame; add a label tier: worlds with depth < -0.25 get .pa-quiet (CSS: hide b/em, keep a 3px brass tick), toggled only on tier CHANGE to avoid classList churn. For the near rank, run a cheap 1D overlap pass each frame on the front planets sorted by x (labels are fixed-width-ish; compare center distance vs estimated width): the deeper of any overlapping pair drops to .pa-quiet for that frame window. Spyglass: on pointermove (already throttled to once per frame via par), find nearest anchor within 140px and set .is-scoped — CSS reuses the existing :hover ruleset (02-hub.css lines 143-147) via a shared selector, plus a ::before hairline leader scaled from the plate toward the dot. Keyboard already has this for free: focus-visible shares the hover rules.

- **First light — onboarding by desire, not instruction** — *◆ strong · effort S*
  No more 'Tab to cycle · Enter to travel.' On first arrival, after the approach settles, the Artifact pings and ONE world flares as the sonar ring touches it — its name and its whispered tell lighting like a struck match. Wait, and a second world beckons. You click because you want to, and you've been taught everything without reading a word.
  *How:* A tiny hub-only state machine on the Ticker in 05-core.js, gated by the same 'orrery-approach' session key: 1.2s after runApproach's finish(), fire pingSonar() and when the ring radius (rings[i] progress is already computed in drawSonar) crosses the frontmost unvisited world, add .is-beckoning to its anchor — CSS makes it share the existing hover ruleset plus one soft 2s ring bloom. Every 8s of continued idle, beckon the next-nearest unvisited world (max 3). Any pointer/keydown kills it, same capture-listener pattern as runApproach's skip. Rewrite .hub-hint copy to diegetic ('the instrument is listening'), and keep one keyboard grace note: the first Tab press lights all pa-rings for 1.5s so cycle order is visible. rm: beckoned world gets the static ring highlight only, mirroring html.rm[data-konami] at 03b-deco.css line 208.

- **The instrument remembers — dusk light and visit evolution** — *· nice · effort M*
  Arrive at dusk and the horizon glows ember behind the ring; at 2am the giants glint colder and comets run more often. Come back a third time and the engraved plate under the sphere reads 'Object 0 · survey ongoing · visit 3'; finish the survey and the hub itself honors you — the orrery face idles with its rings lit gold and your seal riding the HUD.
  *How:* seasonTilt (05-core.js line 98) already reads local time — extend it: at boot compute an hour-bucket and set --horizon on :root (warm #2a1a12 dusk / cold current #0a251e night), which the #orrery gradient in 02-hub.css consumes unchanged; scale buildStars giant frequency and scheduleComet's 24-40s window by the bucket. Persistence: localStorage 'orrery-visits' (wrapped in the existing store try/catch idiom, but localStorage so it outlives the sessionStorage approach key); write visit N into #artifact-label textContent at boot. Post-survey honors: the survey-complete key already exists — on hub boot with it set, ask SphereForge for a lit variant of the orrery skin (bake once: redraw the band canvas with a gold-screen ring overlay) and show the .konami-seal-style monogram beside the survey plate. Unvisited worlds get a slow 6s glint on their pa-ring from visit 2 on — desire pointed at what's left.

- **Warp signatures — every departure speaks its destination** — *◆ strong · effort M*

Same 1.1s, same router, but the journey already smells like where you're going — and arrival lands with a heartbeat of world-hue vignette, like breaking atmosphere.
  *How:* A WARP_MOTES spec table in 05-core.js keyed by slug ({glyph:'char'|'spark'|'flake'|'ember', n:14, hue}): when travel() sets warp.active, seed a pooled 14-slot mote array (reuse the comet pool pattern at line 318) that drawSky advects toward the flood origin with warp.p as its clock — glyph motes draw with fillText from a pre-baked 8-char sprite strip (canvas stays aria-hidden scenery; no meaningful text).
